# PawConnect Product Skill

## Product
PawConnect connects people who want to adopt dogs with people who need to rehome dogs responsibly.

## Roles
Only:
- `user`
- `admin`

A user can adopt and rehome.

## Core journeys
### Adoption
Browse → Dog Details → Apply → Review → Approved → Reserved → Completed → Adopted

### Rehoming
Submit → Pending → Admin Review → Available / Rejected

## Product priorities
1. Trust
2. Fast dog discovery
3. Safe rehoming
4. Transparent application status
5. Simple moderation
6. Strong mobile usability

## Scope
Do not add chat, payments, maps, delivery tracking, AI, staff hierarchies, or complex analytics without explicit approval.

## Content
Use plain human language. Never invent statistics, guarantees, fees, screening claims, or welfare outcomes that the product does not actually enforce or measure.

## UX outcome
A visitor should understand within seconds:
- what PawConnect is
- how to find a dog
- how to rehome a dog
- why the workflow is moderated
